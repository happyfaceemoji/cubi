CUBI v2 SERVICEABLE BODY - PRINT & BUILD NOTES (19 Nov 2026)

WHAT CHANGED
v7 was one shell with every part glued to its walls. Closing it pressed on the wiring and unseated the
Sense board. v2 splits the body in two:
  HOOD    = the v7 shell skin (same eyes, bezels, grille and corner screw bosses). Nothing is mounted to it.
  CHASSIS = base plate + three bulkheads + four servo frames. Every part and wire lives here.
Remove the 4 M2 screws underneath and the hood lifts straight up. No wires go to the hood.

FIRST, PRINT THE COUPONS (cubi_v2_coupons_plate.stl, about 15 min)
  coupon_eyes       front bulkhead strip. Drop the camera module into its slot from the top, push the
                    keeper on, and screw the 30 deg pod on with an M2x4 countersunk screw from the front.
  coupon_eyes_hood  the hood's front wall. Hold it against coupon_eyes and look through the eyes: the lens
                    and sensor should sit centred behind the ovals.
  coupon_hip_v3     one servo corner. The servo goes in from OUTSIDE until its tabs sit on the pads, then
                    its own screws go in from outside.
  Rule unchanged: modelled clearance under 1 mm is a fail.

PRINT (A1 mini, PLA Basic, 0.2 Standard or 0.16 Fine, no supports, about 40-45 g)
  cubi_v2_A1mini_plate_PLA_Basic.stl  hood (top face down), chassis (base down), battery door,
                                      3 sensor pods (30/35/40 deg, front face down), camera keeper
  Hood and chassis: 4 walls, 15% gyroid. Pods and keeper: 4 walls, 40% infill.
  Legs and shoes don't change: use the v2 plate parts you already have.

HARDWARE ADDED
  3x M2x4 countersunk screws: 2 for the battery door, 1 for the sensor pod
  1 mm foam tape: one pad on the back of the XIAO (the mid bulkhead presses it forward and keeps the B2B
  connector seated)
  The existing 4x M2x6 screws hold the hood on.

ASSEMBLY (hood off the whole time)
 1. Servos: slide each one in from the OUTSIDE of its frame, shaft outward, until the tabs sit on the pads.
    Drive the servo's own screws through the tabs into the pads (pre-drill 1 mm). To replace one later:
    take the leg off, take out 2 screws, and pull it straight out. Nothing else moves.
 2. Switch: push the bushing through the rear bulkhead from inside. Its body sits in the 0.6 mm pocket.
    Glue it. The tongue below it fills the hood's slot.
 3. Battery: put it in from UNDERNEATH, leads first through the rear notch. Close the door with 2 screws.
    To replace the battery: open the door and unplug the PH2.0. The rest stays put.
 4. Distance sensor: glue the GY-530 into a pod, chip facing out through the window and SCL/SDA pads toward
    the middle of the robot (wires exit the notch). Screw the pod on from the front of the front bulkhead.
    Start with the 30 deg pod. If `d` on clear desk still reads 150+ mm, swap to 35 or 40. That swap is one
    screw.
 5. Camera + XIAO: stick the foam pad on the back of the XIAO. Lower the camera module into the slot behind
    its eye with the lens in the slot. The board stack drops down between the front and mid bulkheads.
    Push the keeper clip over the top of the slot. To service: pull the keeper and lift the camera and XIAO
    out as one unit.
 6. Speaker: foam-tape it to the rib on the speaker side, sitting on the ledge, sound side out.
    Amp: foam-tape it to the back of the mid bulkhead. Caps: zip-tie them to the slots in the rear bulkhead.
 7. Touch: copper tape on top of the paddle between the rear and mid bulkheads. It works through the 0.3 mm
    gap and the 1.6 mm ceiling. Re-check the touch threshold with `h`.
 8. Antenna: if you fit the U.FL antenna, stick it in the 0.4 mm recess on the back of the rear bulkhead.
 9. Run the self-test with the switch ON, then lower the hood and fit the 4 M2x6 screws from underneath.

WHAT MOVED (check these)
  - The camera module and XIAO sit 1.9 mm further back than in v7. The lens can no longer poke into the
    wall, because the hood has to slide past it. The hood's USB-C opening moved back to match (z 8.2) and
    is 0.4 mm wider. MEASURE how far the lens sticks out past the module face. If it's more than 1.6 mm,
    raise LENS_PROUD and regenerate.
  - The servo top faces sit 0.3 mm further in, so the legs sit 0.3 mm closer to the body.
  - The switch lever sticks out about 1.9 mm less (it now goes through bulkhead + gap + hood).
  - The 45 deg pod was dropped: its beam clips the eye oval. 40 deg clips by 0.02 mm3, which is negligible.
  - Weight: about +9 g of plastic (solid-equivalent 43 g vs 34 g for v7 shell + plate). That's the cost of the
    double walls. Weigh her: grip was already marginal at about 100 g.

FIT CHECK  python fitcheck_v2.py cubi_shell_v7.stl  -> 64/64 pass
  Static clearances; the hood lifting straight up 60 mm past servos, switch and chassis; each servo pulling
  out; camera + XIAO lifting out; battery dropping out; each pod sliding back and lifting out; ToF beam
  (25 deg FoV) vs hood and chassis; camera view cone (26 deg half-angle) vs hood and chassis.
